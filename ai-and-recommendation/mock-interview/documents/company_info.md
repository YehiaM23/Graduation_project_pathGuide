# TechVision Inc. - Company Overview

## About Us
TechVision Inc. is a leading artificial intelligence company founded in 2018, headquartered in San Francisco, California. We specialize in building enterprise AI solutions that transform how businesses operate.

## Mission
To democratize AI technology and make it accessible to every organization, enabling them to make smarter decisions and automate complex workflows.

## Products
- **VisionAI Platform**: Our flagship product - an end-to-end MLOps platform for training, deploying, and monitoring machine learning models at scale.
- **DataSync Pro**: Real-time data integration tool that connects 200+ data sources.
- **InsightEngine**: Natural language analytics dashboard that lets non-technical users query data conversationally.

## Engineering Culture
- We practice agile development with 2-week sprints
- Strong emphasis on code review and testing (minimum 80% coverage)
- Tech stack: Python, TypeScript, React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kubernetes
- We value clean code, documentation, and knowledge sharing
- Weekly tech talks and monthly hackathons

## Team Structure
- 150 employees across engineering, product, sales, and operations
- Engineering is organized into squads: Platform, ML Infrastructure, Frontend, Data, and DevOps
- Each squad has 5-8 engineers, a tech lead, and a product manager

## Benefits
- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-first with optional office in SF and NYC
- Unlimited PTO with minimum 15 days encouraged
- $2,000 annual learning budget
- Health, dental, and vision insurance

## Interview Process
1. Initial phone screen (30 min)
2. Technical assessment / take-home project
3. Technical interview with team members (60 min)
4. System design interview (45 min)
5. Culture fit / behavioral interview (30 min)
6. Final decision within 1 week

## Values
- **Innovation**: We push boundaries and embrace experimentation
- **Transparency**: Open communication at all levels
- **Impact**: We measure success by customer outcomes
- **Growth**: Continuous learning is part of our DNA
- **Collaboration**: The best ideas come from diverse teams working together
